You're in. Let's install.

Thank you for testing Life School Log. These steps take about two minutes on your Android phone.

Install

1
Make sure the Play Store is set to the same Gmail you used to join the group (tap your profile picture in the Play Store to check).
2
Tap the button, then tap Become a tester. Become a tester
3
Tap Download it on Google Play and install.

If it says the app isn't available, your group request may still be waiting, or the Play Store is using a different Gmail.

What we ask

  • Keep the app installed for 14 days in a row. Google requires this before we can publish, so please don't uninstall early.
  • Use it on a few real school days. Log lessons and everyday learning, and try Progress and the Binder.

The paid version

  • Pro is free during the test. Tap Unlock and choose Test card, always approves. You won't be charged.
  • Pro goes back to paid when the app launches. Everything you logged stays.

How to send feedback

Anything that is confusing, broken or missing helps. All of these are private to us.

  1. From the Play Store (easiest): open the Play Store, tap your profile picture > Manage apps & device > Manage > Life School Log. Near the bottom, in the tester section, tap Send private feedback. Screenshots can't be attached there.
  2. By email: write to lifeschoollog@gmail.com and attach screenshots.
  3. Through the group: on the group page, tap Contact owners and managers.
  4. On Instagram: send us a DM.

What helps most: what you were trying to do, what happened and what you expected, a screenshot, and your phone model.

Your records stay on your phone. We never see them.
