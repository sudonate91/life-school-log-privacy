Privacy Policy for Life School Log

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Life School Log is a homeschool record-keeping app. This page explains what happens with your family's information when you use it.

The short version

Life School Log does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything you enter — children's names, photos, activity logs, notes — stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps its data in a private database and file storage on your device, using Android's standard app-private storage. This includes:

  • Child profiles (name, birthday, grade, notes, and an optional photo)
  • Activity and lesson entries (subject, time, and any photos you attach)
  • Settings and preferences, such as your chosen theme and parent-gate PIN

None of this leaves your phone. The app has no way to send it anywhere, because it has no server to send it to and does not request internet access for that purpose.

Backups

If you export a backup file, that file is created and saved wherever you choose to save it — for example, a folder you pick, or a cloud-storage app already installed on your phone. Life School Log does not upload backups itself; any transfer happens only through the storage location or app you choose, and is governed by that service's own privacy practices, not this one.

Photos

Photos you attach to a child's profile or to an activity entry are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, and the app does not access your camera or photo library except when you choose to add a picture.

Reminders and notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Android's local notification system. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Pro features (such as the printable Binder export) are sold as a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the purchase and payment information; Life School Log never sees your payment details. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's information

The app is designed for parents to keep records about their own children as part of homeschooling. That information is entered by the parent, stored only on the parent's own device, and is never collected by us or shared with anyone. We do not operate any service that collects information from children directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and the new version will be posted at the same address.

Contact
